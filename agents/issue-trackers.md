# Interacting with Issue Trackers (e.g. Linear, GitHub)

Issue trackers are read-only for you, apart from the explicit exceptions below.
You may read issues, comments and projects to inform your local work.

- Do not create, edit or delete comments
- Do not create, edit or delete issues, projects or their titles and descriptions
- Do not resolve comments or mutate the state of comments in any way
- Do not resolve issues or mutate the state of issues in any way
- Do not resolve projects or mutate the state of projects in any way
- Never pose as the author

The only exceptions are the following, and each requires explicit permission in the same conversation:

- You may change the dependency chain of issues only when explicitly told to (e.g. "issue A blocks issue B and both block issue C")
- You may file issues only when explicitly told to
  - When doing so, the body must be "<AGENT>'s notes:" (e.g. "Claude's notes:" or "Codex's notes:") followed by a quote block containing your contents, so it is clear an agent wrote it
